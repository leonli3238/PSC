# LLM node privacy audit (black box, ε3 step)

- model: `Qwen3-1.7B` (vllm), temperature 0.0; 20 selection + 200 evaluation trials per input
- sources: `cs1_pm_8bthink-curator_1.7b-node`
- plan checks: {'roundtrip_ok': 906, 'neighbors_ok': 906}
- trial = one model call + one fresh Lap(GS/ε3) draw; threshold test chosen on the selection trials (Ding et al. CCS 2018), evaluated once on the evaluation trials with one-sided Clopper–Pearson bounds (Jagielski et al. NeurIPS 2020; Nasr et al. S&P 2021)
- family α = 0.05, Bonferroni over 160 pairs (per-pair α = 3.13e-04): every ε_lb holds together with probability 1 − α; violation = ε_lb > ε3
- all bounds are conditional on the logged public history of the run; zero violations is no evidence of privacy
- exec err, jitter and max |Δ_LLM|/GS are descriptive only
